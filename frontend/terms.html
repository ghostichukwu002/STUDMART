Terms of use

Plain-language draft for launch. A lawyer should review it before STUDMART goes live.

STUDMART is a campus marketplace where students and campus shops list items for sale. By creating an account or using STUDMART, you agree to these terms.

1. What STUDMART is

STUDMART connects buyers and sellers. We do not own the items listed, we do not take payment, and we do not deliver. Every deal happens directly between buyer and seller.

2. Your account

  • Give accurate information and keep your password private.
  • Student verification means an admin checked a student ID link. It does not guarantee who you are.
  • Shop accounts are for businesses selling on campus. Shops must give a real address and contact number.

3. Listings

  • Only list items you own or have the right to sell.
  • Do not list anything illegal under Nigerian law, including drugs, weapons, stolen goods, counterfeit goods, or adult content.
  • Describe the item honestly, including its condition and price, and use photos of the item itself.
  • Mark a listing as sold once the item has gone.

4. Buying and meeting

  • Meet on campus in a public place, inspect the item before you pay, and do not send money to someone you have not met.
  • STUDMART is not responsible for the quality, safety, or delivery of items, or for disputes between buyers and sellers.

5. Reports, blocking and removal

Anyone can report a listing or a user, and can block a seller. Admins may remove listings and suspend accounts that break these terms. We may do this without notice where needed to keep the community safe.

6. Ending your account

You can stop using STUDMART at any time. We may suspend or close accounts that break these terms.

We may update these terms. We will show the date of the latest version here.
