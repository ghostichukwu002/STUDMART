Privacy notice

Plain-language draft for launch. A lawyer should review it before STUDMART goes live.

This notice explains what STUDMART collects, why, and what you can ask us to do with it.

What we collect

  • Account: first name, last name, email, school or shop details, bio, profile photo, and a password stored only as a scrambled (hashed) value.
  • Listings: titles, descriptions, prices, photos, and the WhatsApp number you add.
  • Student verification: the link to a student ID you share, and any note you add.
  • Safety: reports you make, and blocks you set.
  • Usage: saved listings and basic activity needed to run the site.

Why we use it

To run your account and listings, to check student status when you ask, to keep the marketplace safe, and to send account emails such as password resets.

Student ID links

Only STUDMART admins can see the ID link you share for verification. You can ask us to delete it at any time. Do not share an ID link you are not comfortable having stored.

Who sees what

  • Your name, school or shop name, listings and bio are visible to other users.
  • A WhatsApp number is visible to people viewing your listing, if you add one.
  • Admins can see account details and reports to keep the site safe.

Who helps us run STUDMART

We use hosting providers for the website, the server, and email delivery. We do not sell your personal information.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, or delete your personal information. Send your request to okaforanointing37@gmail.com. Nigeria's Data Protection Act 2023 gives you these rights.

Keeping your data safe

We use HTTPS, hash passwords, and limit repeated sign-in attempts. No online service is perfectly secure, so use a password you do not use elsewhere.

Changes

We will update this notice when our practices change and show the date of the latest version.
